## Opportunity framing

The opportunity identified by this ecosystem is:

> Build a climate-health coordination and assistance platform that uses shared evidence, role-based access, and traceable workflow to connect policy, public-health coordination, healthcare handoff, community support, logistics, resource accountability, and post-event learning.

This opportunity can support four future health-service dimensions:

| Dimension | Future opportunity | Phase 0 boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Promotion | heat literacy, consistent messages, community awareness, and targeted information | information and contact-point concept only |
| Prevention | vulnerable-context review, outreach coordination, facility readiness, and coverage monitoring | mock evidence-to-task workflow |
| Treatment | handoff to healthcare facilities or emergency services | contact-point/escalation concept; no clinical decision support |
| Recovery | post-event review, unresolved-task follow-up, resource learning, and policy improvement | summary and review fields; no health-outcome claim |

Policy, legal requirements, privacy, financing, accessibility, interoperability, and accountability are cross-cutting conditions. They should remain roadmap questions until local authorities, data owners, and stakeholders validate them.

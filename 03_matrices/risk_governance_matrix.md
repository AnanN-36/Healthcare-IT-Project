# Risk and Governance Matrix

**Purpose:** identify risks that could weaken the problem-state argument, mislead users, or make a future live service unsafe.  
**Updated:** 2026-08-21

| Risk or governance issue | Potential consequence | Phase 0 control | Owner for next decision | Evidence / Interpretation / Assumption / Decision | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Historical, forecast, official, and mock data are mixed | Users may treat unlike values as one risk score | Label data status; display source, period, unit, and assumptions | Mod + My + Com | Evidence: Mod, My, and Com require source-aware interpretation. **Decision:** never merge silently. | Open control |
| Unverified heat thresholds | A prototype label may be mistaken for an official warning level | Use illustrative labels; require human confirmation; record threshold source | Mod + June | Evidence: June identifies threshold gap. **Decision:** no official threshold claim in Phase 0. | Open validation |
| Patient-level data or identifiers enter the prototype | Privacy, ethics, and security exposure | Use aggregate or mock records; minimize fields; prohibit names and addresses | Com + whole team | Evidence: Com and Mod set privacy boundary. **Decision:** no identifiable data. | Required control |
| Operational signal is interpreted as clinical diagnosis | Users may infer treatment or individual medical risk | Use `high-priority operational signal`; add scope note; require human review | Mod + Belle | Evidence: Mod defines non-clinical boundary. **Decision:** no diagnosis or treatment feature. | Required control |
| Digital-first delivery excludes high-risk groups | The people most at risk may not receive the message | Treat dashboard as coordination/confirmation destination; include human/independent fallback fields | Chris + stakeholder team | Evidence: Chris identifies digital exclusion and channel failure modes. **Decision:** delivery remains outside dashboard-only scope. | Open validation |
| Fallback shares the same failure mode | A failed grid/network channel may fail repeatedly | Pair digital channel with an independent channel in the concept matrix | Chris | Evidence: Chris channel strategy. **Decision:** fallback independence is a design requirement. | Required control |
| No clear task owner | A signal remains visible but no action follows | Require owner_role and assignee_role before simulated task completion | Belle + Chris | Evidence: Belle task use case; Chris handoff matrix. **Decision:** owner is mandatory field. | Required control |
| Acknowledgement is confused with completion | Team may overestimate response performance | Separate sent, received, acknowledged, action, and completed states | Belle + Chris | Evidence: Chris central finding and Belle task states. **Decision:** separate workflow states. | Required control |
| Data source access, licence, or API is unresolved | Future live implementation may be infeasible | Use file-based ingestion and maintain source/licence notes | My + Com + June | Evidence: My and June identify access/licence gaps. **Decision:** Phase 0 does not depend on live API. | Open dependency |
| Conflicting values from sources | Users may lose trust or make inconsistent decisions | Preserve raw values; add conflict_flag and source comparison note | My + Com | Evidence: My data quality rules. **Decision:** do not overwrite conflicts without policy. | Required control |
| Alert fatigue or unclear prioritization | Users may ignore repeated signals | Use a human-reviewable operational signal and test clarity/actionability | Belle + Chris | Evidence: Chris alert-fatigue review. **Decision:** evaluate comprehension before automation. | Open validation |
| Response time is presented as policy | Arbitrary deadlines may be mistaken for standards | Label timing as simulation parameter to test | June + Chris | Evidence: June and Chris note no validated response window. | Required control |
| Global evidence is overgeneralized to Thailand | Claims may be inaccurate or culturally/operationally unsuitable | Label transferability and assumptions; validate local ownership and channels | Mod + June + Chris | Evidence: Chris cautions against treating international analogies as Thai evidence. | Open validation |
| Interoperability standards are over-scoped | Team may spend Phase 0 effort on production integration | Mention FHIR/LOINC/SNOMED/ICD-11 as future direction only | Com | Evidence: Com review. **Decision:** not MVP requirements. | Deferred |
| Phase 0 claims health impact | Pitch may overclaim prototype value | Limit evaluation to feasibility, usability, traceability, and workflow measures | June + whole team | Evidence: June and Chris evaluation boundaries. **Decision:** no mortality or hospital outcome claims. | Required control |
| Audit trail is absent | Team cannot explain data transformation or simulated actions | Record source, import, actor, action, timestamp, and comment | Com + My | Evidence: Com architecture/governance and My provenance fields. | Required control |

# Stakeholder and Contact-Point Matrix

**Purpose:** show the surrounding handoffs that make the Heat Risk Dashboard operationally meaningful. The dashboard is the main component; the other five components remain contact-point level in Pitch 1.  
**Updated:** 2026-08-21

| Contact point | Sender / upstream role | Receiver / downstream role | Information passed | Channel or fallback concept | Phase 0 field | Evidence / Interpretation / Assumption / Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Data source to dashboard | meteorological or health-data source | HeatShield data layer | indicator, value, unit, timestamp, geography, quality metadata | file import or source adapter; manual review fallback | source, timestamp, freshness, quality_flag | Evidence: My and Com describe source-aware ingestion. **Decision:** simulate/file-import in Phase 0. |
| Dashboard to coordinator | HeatShield dashboard | public-health or healthcare coordinator | trend, area context, vulnerable context, caveat | dashboard review; human confirmation | signal_status, reviewer, rationale | Evidence: Belle and Mod define primary user and interpretation boundary. |
| Coordinator to action owner | coordinator | CHW, facility contact, local role | task, area, priority, action note, due state | role-addressed channel; phone or human fallback | owner, assignee, status | Evidence: Belle task use case; Chris handoff matrix. **Assumption:** exact local owner requires validation. |
| Institutional alert to facility | health authority or coordinator | facility contact | heat signal, preparation/status request | dashboard/email/SMS concept; telephone fallback | recipient, channel, acknowledgement | Evidence: Chris matrix. **Decision:** show contact point only, not clinical surge protocol. |
| Local coordination to CHW | local health office | community health worker | priority area, outreach task, household/contact context | human contact or role channel; local fallback | task_type, priority, fallback_path | Evidence: Chris Thailand notes. **Assumption:** heat-specific CHW/อสม. role requires local confirmation. |
| CHW or local role to household | CHW or local contact | household, caregiver, or resident | public guidance or check-in instruction | human contact, phone, community channel | delivery_channel, acknowledged | Evidence: Chris finds digital reach may exclude high-risk groups. **Decision:** do not make dashboard the only delivery channel. |
| Recipient acknowledgement to coordinator | CHW, facility, local role | coordinator/dashboard | received, understood, accepted, unable, or no response | return channel; independent fallback | acknowledgement_state, time | Evidence: Chris distinguishes transmission, receipt, and action. **Decision:** first-class field. |
| Unacknowledged task to fallback owner | dashboard/coordinator | backup role or channel owner | overdue or unacknowledged task | independent-failure fallback | fallback_owner, escalation_state | Evidence: Chris requires independent failure mode. **Assumption:** timing is a Phase 0 parameter. |
| Action status to coordinator | task owner | coordinator | in progress, completed, unable, unresolved | dashboard status update or confirmation | task_status, completion_note | Evidence: Belle user stories; Chris handoff logic. |
| End-period review | coordinator/evaluator | team and stakeholders | completed, overdue, unresolved, acknowledgement, errors | dashboard summary or export | review_period, unresolved_count, lessons | Evidence: June and Chris evaluation. **Decision:** include in prototype summary. |
| Feedback to product team | users and stakeholders | HeatShield team | usefulness, clarity, trust, actionability, barriers | survey, interview, task test | rating, comment, role | Evidence: Chris CSAT-style evaluation. **Decision:** use for feasibility only. |
